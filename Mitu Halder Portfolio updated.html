Final Year Design Project (FYDP)

“One Face Is All You Need: GenAI-Augmented Enrollment for One-Shot Biometric Authentication”

Ongoing research project

I’m researching the use of Generative AI to improve one-shot face recognition by generating diverse facial images from a single reference image. The central idea is to use only one original face image during enrollment and explore GenAI-generated face variations to support authentication.

The goal is to improve recognition performance under different lighting conditions, facial angles and expressions.
